# AgentTrust NEAR video brief

**Audience:** NEAR hackathon judges seeing the product for the first time, then developers checking proof. **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC MP4, approximately 128 seconds, readable on a phone. **Opening:** four fast, labeled results from dated evidence: the separately executed r4-16 A refusal, its no-payout docs row, the saved model B final view, and the fully checked 15-row synthetic report. **Goal:** prove the risk and useful outcome first, then show the actual site, owner boundary, saved model/MCP path, native receipt and separate r4-17/r4-18 exact retry.

The [official event instructions](https://nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md) require a video for local-only demos, a public reproducible repo, and a human X demo post. They do not prescribe length or voice. [X's non-Premium cap](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-videos) is 140 seconds and 512 MB, so retain margin. The [AgentTrust Labs pinned post](https://x.com/agenttrustlabs/status/2054278036120637541) supplies the concise risk-first voice, but its old Solana claims do not carry over. The [NEARCON winner obrigado.cc submission](https://devpost.com/software/obrigado) uses a short task-to-payout walkthrough; this is an example, not a scoring rule.

## Editorial rules

Use the existing white/ink/violet AgentTrust design, large text, 48px fixed bottom-center captions and source labels. The label changes at every opening cut to separate the direct A refusal from the saved model B outcome and checked synthetic report. Show the actual product site, dated docs stills, and the two existing read-only Decisions expansion clips. The owner slide reveals exact identities and required credential first, the 0.01 NEAR cap second, and the restricted worker path third. The report slide reveals the 15-row and 8/7 result with the spoken line; its fixed-input checker note appears last. A fourth layout enlarges only the r4-17/r4-18 rows from the captured docs table. The site's original hero loop supplies motion on the landing shot; a guided crop points to real site copy and the Scoped worker-key panel. Crops and callouts are editorial graphics over saved captures; they do not simulate new site interaction, a model request or a chain payment. No raw `.agenttrust` files, keys, tokens, personal data, or invented model dialogue appear on screen.

Audio must have public-use rights before export. The provided ElevenLabs key checked as free tier; [ElevenLabs limits free output to non-commercial use with attribution](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/billing), so this promotional cut uses the locally generated [Apache-2.0 Kokoro model](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) and its `af_heart` voice. Captions are aligned to that actual narration. The cut has no face circle, purchased credits or live-chain calls.

## Completion checks

1. Match voice to picture and fixed subtitles; verify the useful audit result, model B purchase, separate executed A refusal and separate exact retry are unmistakable.
2. Decode the whole MP4; check 1920×1080/30 fps/H.264/AAC, duration below 140 seconds, file below 512 MB, first 10 seconds, scene boundaries and final frame.
3. Inspect at phone scale for legibility, subtitle timing, distinct attempt labels, receipts, provenance and accidental secrets. Have independent first-time-judge and technical-judge reviews; repair their concrete findings.
4. Commit the reviewed T29 package and push private. X posting and official submission remain separate actions.
